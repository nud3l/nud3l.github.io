## About Me

Hi, I'm Dominik Harz (most people call me Dom), a computer scientist and entrepreneur. I'm passionate about building decentralized systems that enable people to collaborate and build better societies.

## My Work

I'm a co-founder of [BOB](https://gobob.xyz) ("Build on Bitcoin"), a hybrid L2 that brings the innovation of Ethereum to Bitcoin. Currently focused on:

- **BOB Gateway**: Programmable Bitcoin swaps across chains
- **Native Vaults**: Better BTC custody and DeFi integrations
- **AI**: Improving how teams build and collaborate together

I did my PhD at the [Imperial College London cryptocurrency lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/cryptocurrency) with [William Knottenbelt](https://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~wjk/) where I met some of the greatest people I had the chance to work with on DeFi and its security, cross-chain protocols, and smart contracts. During my PhD, I co-authored [XCLAIM](https://xclaim.io), a trustless cross-chain bridge protocol that was built as [Interlay](https://interlay.io). To get an idea of my work, please have a look at my [publications](/publications).
